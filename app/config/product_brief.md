# Dreamcraftr Product Brief

## 1. Core Overview & Mission

- **Product Name & URL:** Dreamcraftr (`dreamcraftr.com`)
- **One-Liner / Tagline:**

  > **Describe it. We dream it.**
  > Share your vision, shape your characters, set your scenes — and watch your imagination transform into a beautifully illustrated storybook that takes readers on a journey.

- **Core Problem Solved:**
  - **Bedtime & Routine Friction:** Turning chaotic bedtime routines into an engaging, bonding tradition.
  - **Screen-Time Alternatives:** Providing interactive, high-value physical and digital reading experiences that compete with passive screen time.
  - **Generic Storytelling:** Moving beyond basic name-insertion to full creative control over characters, settings, and storylines.
  - **Lesson Engagement in Classrooms:** Helping educators create custom stories that directly teach targeted social-emotional or academic concepts using relatable scenarios.
  - **Brand/Marketing Narrative:** Allowing creators and marketers to produce tailored, child-friendly narrative assets that communicate ideas effectively to young audiences.
- **Target Audience & Key Personas:**
  1. **The Engaged Parent (ages 25-42):** Parents of young children (ages 2-10) seeking meaningful bedtime stories, screen-free bonding, and personal development tools (e.g., stories about moving schools, learning bravery, or welcoming a sibling).
  2. **The Thoughtful Gift-Giver:** Grandparents, godparents, and relatives looking for unique, sentimental gifts for birthdays, holidays, or milestones.
  3. **Educators & Teachers (K-3 / early childhood):** Teachers who want to generate custom, targeted stories for classroom lessons, social-emotional learning (SEL), conflict resolution, or teaching specific history/science topics through narrative.
  4. **Children's Authors & Self-Publishers:** Aspiring creators who want to quickly prototype, visualize, and outline illustrated children's storybooks.
  5. **Marketers, Brands & Content Creators:** Businesses, non-profits, or creators targeting family/child demographics who need custom illustrated stories, educational branded content, or custom promotional storybooks.

---

## 2. Brand Identity & Voice Guidelines

- **Brand Tone & Personality:**
  - **Warm & Whimsical:** Inviting, imaginative, and magical without being overly childish or chaotic.
  - **Empowering & Creative:** Focuses on user co-creation ("You share the vision, we bring it to life").
  - **Trustworthy & Heartfelt:** Grounded in early childhood value, family bonding, classroom engagement, and literacy.
- **Key vocabulary and themes to emphasize:** imagination, core memories, bedtime bonding, custom worlds, hero of the story, visual storytelling, bespoke adventures, screen-free magic, creative vision.
- **Words & tropes to avoid (strict rules for the strategy agent):**
  - Never mention "AI" or tech mechanics directly. Avoid words like "AI," "AI-generated," "artificial intelligence," "algorithm," "LLM," "prompt," "prompt engineering," "synthetic generation," or "automation."
  - **Framing rule:** frame the product as an empowering creative engine/platform, not a software utility. Use terms like "crafted," "dreamed," "brought to life," "illustrated," and "tailored."
  - Avoid corporate or clinical jargon. Keep language evocative, warm, and clear.

---

## 3. Product Mechanics & Features

- **Core features:**
  - **Photo-to-Character Creation:** Upload photos to automatically model characters after real children, family members, or pets.
  - **Diverse Art Style Selection:** Choose from multiple distinct illustration styles (e.g., watercolor, 3D animated, comic, classic storybook) to match the narrative vibe.
  - **Frictionless Story Generation:** A straightforward, effortless flow that turns simple ideas into polished books in minutes with minimal effort required.
  - **Full Plot & Scene Customization:** Tailor storylines to specific lessons, hobbies, adventures, or milestones.
  - **Digital Reading & Physical Hardcovers:** Instant online access to read interactively on any screen, plus high-quality printed hardcovers shipped directly to your door.
- **The simple 3-step process (for social hooks & how-to posts):**
  1. **Upload & Describe:** Upload a photo, pick an art style, and set the scene or lesson in a few simple clicks.
  2. **We Dream It:** Dreamcraftr seamlessly crafts the storyline and custom artwork with zero heavy lifting required.
  3. **Share & Read:** Enjoy the digital storybook instantly online or order a hardcover for your bookshelf.

## 4. Content Pillars & Social Strategy Angles

- **Pillar 1 - "The Magic of Being the Hero" (emotional & bonding):** focus on the psychological and emotional impact when a child sees themselves - rendered in beautiful art - inside a book. Topics: reading motivation, bedtime bonding, building self-esteem, bedtime routine hacks.
- **Pillar 2 - "Effortless Creativity" (product demo & showcase):** show how fast and simple it is to go from a quick idea or photo to a full storybook. Topics: "watch me turn a photo of my kid into a fantasy book in 60 seconds," style comparisons (watercolor vs. 3D), unboxing hardcover copies.
- **Pillar 3 - "Tailored Learning & Classroom Wins" (educator & parent solutions):** solve real behavioral or educational challenges through custom stories. Topics: teaching SEL, first-day-of-school anxiety, custom classroom reading materials, brand storytelling for young audiences.
- **Pillar 4 - "Gift of Core Memories" (gifting & seasonal):** position Dreamcraftr as the thoughtful, high-sentimental-value gift that won't end up in the trash. Topics: birthday gifts, holiday gift guides, milestone keepsakes (e.g., graduating preschool, becoming a big brother/sister).

## 6. Key Instructions for the Strategy Agent

1. **Strict terminology rule:** never use words like "AI," "AI-generated," "algorithms," "prompts," or "synthetic." Always frame the product as a creative engine, platform, or storymaker ("crafted," "dreamed," "brought to life").
2. **Platform tone matching:**
   - **TikTok / Instagram Reels:** casual, visual-first, high energy, parent/educator relatable.
   - **Pinterest:** aesthetic cover showcases, gift guides, bedtime routine infographics, classroom activity ideas.
   - **LinkedIn / X (Twitter):** educational value, marketing narrative applications, brand storytelling, and creator tools.
3. **Product accuracy:** never mention PDF downloads or exports. Emphasize instant web reading and printed physical hardcovers.
4. **Generate fresh, don't recycle:** treat section 5's hooks/CTAs as tone reference only - always produce a hook and CTA specific to the campaign brief you were given, never copy an example verbatim.
